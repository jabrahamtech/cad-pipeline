PRINT IN NUMBER ORDER. Filenames carry everything you need.

  _x2         print TWO of it
  _SUPPORTS   the only part that wants supports
  _BRIM       needs a brim
  everything else: SUPPORTS OFF

PETG, 0.2 layers, 3 walls, 15-20% infill.
Same profile the gauge is printed on, or the fits mean nothing.

20 pieces total, ~245 g.

TWO PLACES TO STOP
  after 01  measure the gauge. It settles five numbers the parts are cut
            to. If one is wrong, 02, 04, 06, 07 and 15 get reprinted.
  after 04  check J1 actually turns before committing the rest.

NOTES ON THE AWKWARD ONES
  02  base down, cheeks and posts UP. Supports off - the stub axle bridge
      is load-bearing and support scars it.
  03  the bore IS the J1 journal. Do not sand it.
  04  -X face down. Both journal bores come out vertical and round.
  05  125 mm long. Check it fits your bed before slicing.
  06  supports under the +X wall and both lobes.
  08  needs heat-set inserts.
  11  clamshell - one design, printed twice, second one turned over.
  15  TOP FACE DOWN. Layers must lie along the leaf or the flexure
      delaminates. This is not a preference.

BOUGHT PARTS
  1 x MG995, 2 x MG90S, 3 x SG90, 3 x 608ZZ, M3 hardware,
  heat-set inserts for 08.
