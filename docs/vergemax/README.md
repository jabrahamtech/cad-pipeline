# vergemax — amaran Verge Max on a Neewer ST-200 light stand

Current design: `pole_adapter` — the desk clamp's pole unscrews from its
base, and this puck replicates the base's female thread on top of a 5/8"
stud receiver, so the pole screws straight onto the stand. The earlier
`clamp_bar` (fake desk edge gripped by the whole desk clamp) is kept as a
fallback if the printed fine thread disappoints.

## Assembly (pole_adapter)
1. Drop the 1/4"-20 nut down the threaded top opening and finger-seat it
   into the hex pocket (15 mm down). The nut stays captive — nothing is
   tightened from the top.
2. Spin the whole adapter onto the stand's 1/4"-20 tip until it bottoms
   on the stud shoulder — snug firmly by twisting the puck.
3. (Optional) heat-set the M3 insert into the rear flat, run the M3 screw
   in against the stud to lock rotation.
4. Unscrew the pole from the desk-clamp base and screw it into the
   adapter's top thread until its shoulder seats.

Load path: the light's moment passes through the printed thread into the
steel stud and the stand shoulder. Keep one stand leg under the light's
side.
